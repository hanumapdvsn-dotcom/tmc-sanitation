Mandatory Waste Segregation Notice:
Handing over unsegregated or mixed municipal solid waste is strictly prohibited. Every household is legally required to segregate waste into 4 streams (Wet, Dry, Sanitary & Domestic Hazardous/Special Care).
Legal Reference:
• Rule 4(1), Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 (Duties of Waste Generators)
• Tadipatri Municipality Solid Waste Handling By-Laws.
Non-compliance will lead to refusal of waste collection and imposition of municipal spot fines.
